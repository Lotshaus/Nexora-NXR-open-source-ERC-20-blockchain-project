What is Nexora?

Nexora is an experimental blockchain project created to explore ERC-20 tokens, smart contracts, decentralized technologies and open-source development.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is NXR?

NXR is the ERC-20 token of the Nexora project. It currently exists on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet.

Does NXR have real-world value?

No. The current Nexora deployment is a testnet experiment and should not be treated as a real-world investment or financial product.

Can I inspect the contract?

Yes. The contract is publicly verifiable on the Ethereum Sepolia network.

Is Nexora on mainnet?

No. The current version is deployed on Ethereum Sepolia testnet.
